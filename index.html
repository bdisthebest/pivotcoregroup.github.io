Not a generic tool. A model tuned to the formats your providers and counterparties actually send, running where your data is allowed to live.

Every value is scored against its source and checked for consistency. Anything uncertain stops and waits for a person. This is the part that matters.

Handover with documentation, then ongoing support if formats change or volumes grow.

A recent engagement

An Oklahoma operator with eleven wells on a western Oklahoma asset was preparing federal royalty reporting by hand, working from monthly statements sent by two midstream providers in two different formats.

We built the pipeline that reads both formats, extracts the volumes, prices, and deductions, checks each value against the statement it came from, and holds anything inconsistent for review before submission. The model runs inside the client’s own environment, so no statement leaves their control.
